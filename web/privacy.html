In short

Faithful parents — the app with the devotionals Like a Father and As a Mother Comforts (in German Wie ein Vater and Wie eine Mutter tröstet) — has no accounts, no statistics, no advertising and no tracking. It sets no cookies and builds no profiles. Your reading progress and settings stay on your device. Data goes only to the services named below, and only as far as each feature needs.

Hosting and narration (GitHub Pages)

The web version of the app and the recorded narration are served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open the web version, or when the app plays or downloads a recorded reading, GitHub processes the technical data it needs to deliver the files and secure its service, in particular your IP address, the date and time, the address requested and details of your browser or device. The texts of the iPhone app are stored in the app itself; it contacts GitHub only for the narration.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is delivering the app and its narration securely and reliably. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). See GitHub's privacy statement.

Data on your device

Your reading plans, the days you have marked as read, the last day you opened, your language, text size, font, light or dark setting, reminder time and voice settings are stored on your device, as is narration you download for offline listening. The web version also keeps a copy of itself for offline reading and remembers if you closed the hint about installing it. This data does not leave your device. Storing it is strictly necessary for features you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You delete it under Settings → Progress, by clearing the website's data in your browser, or by removing the app.

Reminder and reading aloud

If you switch on the daily reminder in the iPhone app, the notification is scheduled by your iPhone itself; nothing is sent to us or to any server. When the app reads with your device's own voice, the speech is produced on the device by its operating system.

Links to Bible passages

Each day can link to its Bible passage: in English on BibleGateway.com, in German on bibleserver.com. Data goes to these sites only when you tap such a link; the passage then opens in your browser, and the site's own privacy policy applies.

Contact by email

If you write to us by email, we process your email address and the content of your message in order to reply. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry concerns working together, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is answering your enquiry. We delete messages once they have been dealt with and no retention duty applies.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Write to the email address above. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), the competent one being the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.
