Privacy

Like a Father, As a Mother collects no personal data.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your reading progress, your chosen plans and your text size are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and deleting the app deletes them.

The only time the app uses the internet is when you tap a link to read a Bible passage on BibleGateway.com, which then opens in your browser under that site's own privacy policy.

Questions about this policy: use the contact link on the app's App Store page.
